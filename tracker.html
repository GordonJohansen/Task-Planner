Your projects live in this browser. Download a backup now and then so you never lose them, and restore from a file on a new machine.

Choose where backups are saved. By default they go to your browser's Downloads folder. On Edge or Chrome you can pick a specific folder (for example a OneDrive folder) so backups land there directly and sync offsite.
